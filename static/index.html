Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nowcasting? +
Nowcasting is the detailed, short-term prediction (0-6 hours) of rapidly evolving weather phenomena like severe thunderstorms, heavy rainfall, and hail.
Where does the data come from? +
Currently, this prototype uses synthetic storm simulations for demonstration. The final version will ingest live IMD Doppler Weather Radar (DWR) and satellite data.
What do the map colors indicate? +
Green: Light to moderate rain (10-30 dBZ).
Yellow: Heavy rain (30-40 dBZ).
Red: Severe thunderstorms (40-50 dBZ).
Purple: Extreme weather/Hail (>50 dBZ).
